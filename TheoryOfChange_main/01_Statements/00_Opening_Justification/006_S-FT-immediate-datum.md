# Immediate datum — the lowest trustworthy articulation at the opening is that processive happening is taking place now

## Claim
Under the opening discipline, the lowest trustworthy articulation is not yet static being, finished process, object-transition, or state-space change. It is simpler and closer:

> **processive happening is taking place now.**

This project retains **change** as its broad family-name, but at the opening the nearest lower-bound articulation is **happening / unfolding / occurring** understood as active taking-place rather than as a bare event-label.

## Why this lower-bound is needed
If the opening says too little — for example, “being is” — it risks blankness. Static being is too weak to capture the lived immediacy of enacted inquiry.

If the opening says too much — for example, “fully structured state-transition” — it imports more articulation than has yet been earned.

So the opening needs a narrower middle:
- not blank static presence,
- not already state-transition theory,
- but live processive happening.

## Why this is immediate
Under maximal doubt one may suspend:
- world hypotheses,
- thick self-descriptions,
- metaphysical systems,
- and completed conceptual frameworks.

But if doubting is enacted at all, then something is already taking place. The opening does not yet know the full nature of that taking-place, but it cannot coherently deny its presence while the inquiry is being carried.

## Why this is stronger than bare eventhood
The datum is not merely that some fact is true or some event can be named. The datum is that inquiry is encountered as ongoing enactment. A merely static event-label would underdescribe what survives maximal doubt. What survives is not just “there is something” but “something is occurring.”

That matters because rival views can try to reinterpret occurrence as:
- bare eventhood,
- a static fact inside a completed block,
- or a contentful presentation whose apparent becoming is not ontologically basic.

The opening resists that thinner reading. The datum at stake is already active taking-place.

## Why the project still keeps the word “change”
The project keeps **change** because later files will articulate several richer, equally true aspects of the same underlying reality. But at the opening the concept “change” is already a stabilized conceptual handle. The immediate contact-point is thinner:
- there is live taking-place,
- there is non-flat occurrence,
- there is unfolding rather than blank stasis.

That is why the opening may say “happening” more safely than a thicker notion of change while still staying inside the change-family.

## Multiple true aspect-descriptions
Different descriptions can be true without being rivals if they track different aspects of the same primitive. The point is not to select one magically exclusive word. The point is to say which articulation is doing which job.

At the opening:
- **happening / unfolding / occurring** name the closest lower-bound articulation.
- **change** remains the broader family-name under which later richer articulations can be gathered.

The problem would only arise if the route silently slid from one role to another.

## What this establishes
1. The opening may trust that processive happening is taking place now.
2. This is stronger than blank being and weaker than full structured change.
3. The project may retain “change” as the broader family-name while treating happening/unfolding as the nearest opening articulation.

## What this does not yet establish
It does **not** yet establish:
- full state-transition structure,
- carry, trace, recurrence, or operative articulation,
- time, space, or metric order,
- or the later structured senses of change.
