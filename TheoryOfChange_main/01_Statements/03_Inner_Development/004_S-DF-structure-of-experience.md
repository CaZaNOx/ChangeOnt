# Structure of experience — co-defined triad of subject, awareness, and experience
## Claim (formal)
Subject, awareness, and experience are not three unrelated primitives but three inseparable moments of one minimal lived structure. None is intelligible in full isolation from the others.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
The inner route does not begin with a ready-made ego confronting ready-made objects through a detachable awareness-light. What is first present is one lived structure whose poles can later be distinguished as subject, awareness, and experience.

## Philosophical Justification
The separate files for subject, awareness, and experience prevent compression, but they are not meant to imply three independent substances. Each term leans on the others: subject without lived givenness becomes empty, awareness without subject and experience becomes contentless, and experience without a lived pole and openness relation loses its sense as experience. So the right first-layer result is an explicitly co-defined triad.

## Explanation (informal)
This file closes the minimal inner opening. The triad is one lived structure whose aspects can be named separately for clarity, but whose separation is analytic rather than ontologically primitive.

## Derivation (Philosophical)
- Subject gives the minimal pole of lived givenness.
- Awareness gives the lived relation of openness.
- Experience gives the lived content/occurring.
- None is complete without the others.
- Therefore the minimal inner structure is a co-defined triad rather than three isolated primitives.

## Derivation (Formal/Logical/Mathematical)
```text
InnerTriad = <S, A, E>
with the constraint that subject-pole, awareness-relation, and experience-content co-occur in the minimal lived structure.
```

## Clarifications / Further Context
- The triad is not yet a full consciousness theory.
- It is the minimal inner structure from which richer recursive, mnemonic, and semantic development can later proceed.
- This file also makes explicit that the prior ordering of the three files was expository.

- derive the earliest minimal grounds for subject-formation and memory.
