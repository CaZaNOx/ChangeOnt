# The DS/RDS opening licenses a starting point and a route, not a full subject-metaphysics

## Claim
The DS/RDS construction licenses only the following:
1. a disciplined epistemic start,
2. a live lower bound of occurring experience,
3. and a route from that lower bound into later ontological development.

It does **not** yet license a full metaphysics of subjectivity, idealism, or the claim that all reality is only what is given to the doubting subject.

## Why this scope clarification is needed
Without a scope limit, readers are likely to make one of two mistakes:
- treat the opening as if it already proved idealism,
- or dismiss it as “merely subjective” and therefore irrelevant to ontology.

Both are wrong.

The opening uses the subject-position because ontology must become answerable to some site of intelligibility. But the project does not stop there. The opening is a route into ontology, not the final ontology itself.

## Argument
1. Any ontology meant to be understood must be answerable to some site of inquiry or intelligibility.
2. DS/RDS provide a maximally disciplined version of such a site.
3. What is secured there has special epistemic force, because it survives maximal critical pressure.
4. But surviving such pressure does not by itself prove that all reality is exhausted by what is given there.
5. Therefore the opening has ontological importance without collapsing into idealism.

## Consequence for the chain
The first segment uses DS/RDS to justify the starting primitive.
After that, the chain deliberately widens into ontological continuation:
- outer formation,
- inner development,
- and later their convergence.

So the DS/RDS method is neither disposable nor final. It is a rigor-giving entrance.

## Objections and replies
### Objection 1: “If the opening begins from lived occurrence, isn’t the ontology just phenomenology?”
No. It begins from what cannot be responsibly denied in inquiry. That gives epistemic privilege, not exhaustive ontological reduction.

### Objection 2: “If the opening does not prove idealism, why use subject-language at all?”
Because inquiry is enacted, not free-floating. The question is not whether the route has a subject-position, but how thin and disciplined that position is.

## What this file establishes
- DS/RDS opening has ontological relevance,
- but does not yet settle full subject-metaphysics,
- and should not be read as a final idealist doctrine.

## What this file does not yet establish
- the full inner ontology,
- external reality,
- or the full bridge from epistemic privilege to ontological primacy.
