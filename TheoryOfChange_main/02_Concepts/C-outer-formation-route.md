# Outer formation route
Overview:
This route derives the earliest world-forming structure from change as primitive without smuggling ready-made substances, objecthood, or classical space-time. Its key early burden is to show how local reshaping becomes **shape-sensitive continuation**: some local formations remain flat or vanish quickly, while some bias, reinforce, or stabilize later reshaping strongly enough to ground identity-like persistence.

Current closure note:
The early bridge `001–003` should now be read as conceptually closed at a minimal level: continuation in a locally persistent structured regime occurs through local shape, this yields selective residue where some differentiations matter more than others, and minimal trace is earned when such differentiated shaping can later matter again. What remains open is not whether there is any carried shaping at all, but where exactly reinforcement becomes stable identity in change.


Shape/space consolidation note:
`022A` is now the canonical consolidation point for local shape, CO-space, directed unfolding, coarseness, point-as-ball, and dynamic shape update as a target. Earlier uses of “shape” in `001–003` are thin and provisional: present local differentiation that can matter for continuation. They should not be read as already importing the full dynamic-shape/coarseness doctrine.
