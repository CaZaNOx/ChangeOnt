# Kernel geometric primitives from localized comparability — bridge from bounded local comparison to bend and gauge
## Claim (formal)
Bend and gauge are justified as **kernel-resolution primitives** when bounded local comparison has already yielded two repeatedly needed operator roles: deformation burden and transport/alignment across changing frames. They are not claimed as the earliest ontological distinctions after change.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
Once a bounded local structure must repeatedly compare continuations, track how much one continuation must deform into another, and keep those comparisons coherent across frame-shift, it needs compact reusable handles. Bend and gauge are those handles at kernel resolution.

## Philosophical Justification
[[S-DF-border-localization]] and [[S-DF-localreach-topology]] establish that comparison is local and bounded. [[S-DF-local-comparability-field]] yields a field of preservation, admissibility, or transformation burden. [[S-DF-gauge-alignment-field]] shows that these local judgments require transport/alignment across frame shift. At that point the kernel repeatedly needs two compact handles: one for deformation burden (bend) and one for transport/alignment (gauge). By the primitive-admission criterion, these qualify as kernel primitives because they are irreducible at kernel resolution, recur across multiple downstream mechanisms, and the kernel cannot proceed without them once local comparison is admitted.

## Explanation (informal)
This bridge is about promotion, not about deepest ontology. Bend and gauge are not the earliest concepts after change; they are the first reusable computational handles for geometric/comparative structure at the kernel’s operating resolution.

## Derivation (Philosophical)
- Bounded localization means live comparisons are partial and local.
- Local comparability yields repeated judgments of lesser/greater deformation burden.
- Frame shift yields repeated need for transport/alignment of those judgments.
- A finite kernel needs compact reusable handles for these two recurring roles.
- Therefore bend and gauge are justified as kernel-resolution primitives.

## Derivation (Formal/Logical/Mathematical)
```text
local bounded comparison -> recurring deformation burden role -> bend handle
local bounded comparison + frame shift -> recurring transport/alignment role -> gauge handle

At kernel resolution these recurring roles are treated as primitives because they are reusable and irreducible for the kernel, even though they are downstream of richer ontological structure.
```
