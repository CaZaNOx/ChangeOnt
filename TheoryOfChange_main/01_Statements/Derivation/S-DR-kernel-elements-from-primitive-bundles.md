# Kernel elements from primitive bundles — bridge from operational distinctions to ontological composites
## Claim (formal)
Kernel elements are legitimate when they bundle lower-level primitive distinctions into reusable ontological roles rather than merely collecting heuristics.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
An element is not just a pile of scores. It is a coherent operational face of some real structural role the ontology makes thinkable.

## Philosophical Justification
[[S-DR-kernel-primitives-from-invariant-regimes]] justifies the recurrence/invariance-facing primitive family, while [[S-DR-kernel-geometric-primitives-from-localized-comparability]] justifies bend/gauge primitives from bounded local comparison and alignment transport. Elements become legitimate one level later: when several primitive distinctions from these families must be coordinated to express a recognizable role such as adaptive gauge, identity maintenance, growth under pressure, or motif composition. This is why elements are composites and why their code is only one implementation of the role.

## Derivation (Philosophical)
- EA bundles gauge + temporal adaptation into history-adaptive warp.
- EB bundles pressure, compressibility, and variable birth into disciplined growth.
- EC bundles ReID, closure, and continuity diagnostics into admitted identity-through-change.
- EI bundles motif and quotient operations into change-composition structure.
- EG/EH/EJ bundle precision, search-shape, and order-sensitive control.
- EF remains weaker: it bundles routing and regime control, but its ontological necessity is still less secure than the others.

## Clarifications / Further Context
- This bridge does not erase the difference between strongly justified and provisional elements.
- Vote bridge and CommitmentSurface are operational surfaces required for runtime composition, but they sit closer to kernel architecture than to first-order ontology.

## Next Steps in Chain
- each element file should state why its primitive bundle is irreducible and what failure appears if it is removed.
