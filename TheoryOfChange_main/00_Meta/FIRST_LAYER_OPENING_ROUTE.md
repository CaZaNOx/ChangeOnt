# First-Layer Opening Route

This note fixes the closure condition for the first opening segment of `TheoryOfChange_main`.

## Goal

The opening route should leave a serious reader at this conclusion:

> Change is the strongest justified primitive because it is epistemically unavoidable, because rival starts are weaker or exception-laden, and because beginning from change is therefore more coherent than beginning from substance, static being, passive monism, or timeless totality.

This route should be **pressure-resistant**, not merely suggestive.

## What the opening must do

### A. Shared critical ground
The route begins from what the reader must already grant in reading and reflecting:
- present occurring,
- experiencing/doubting/appearing,
- performative instability of denying change,
- maximal doubt bottoms out somewhere.

### B. Comparative critique
The route must explicitly challenge rival starts, not only mention them.
At minimum, it should pressure:
- existence/being posed statically,
- substance-first ontology,
- passive or merely neutral monism,
- process if process means more than happening,
- block or fixed-possibility-space pictures that demote actualization,
- exempt-absolute or ex nihilo models that require privileged exceptions.

### C. Handoff condition
The opening is complete only when the text can truthfully say:

> We are now justified in taking change as the privileged ontological starting point and asking what follows if change is primitive.

## Anti-bloat rule

The opening should not yet import later machinery unless forced:
- not metric packaging,
- not heavy pointer machinery,
- not rich subject metaphysics,
- not kernel engineering.

Its task is justification of the primitive, not premature completion of the whole system.
