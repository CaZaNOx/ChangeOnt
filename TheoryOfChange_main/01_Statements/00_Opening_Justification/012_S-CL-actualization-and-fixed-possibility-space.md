# A fixed possibility-space or timeless totality cannot keep actualization real while treating it as ontologically cheap

## Claim
If actualization is real, then a merely fixed possibility-space or completed timeless totality is not ontologically sufficient by itself. One cannot coherently admit real occurring while treating the mechanism, constraints, and products of actualization as ontologically negligible.

## Why this matters
Many rival pictures try to say something like:
- all possibilities are already there,
- or the whole history is already equally real,
- and what happens now is only one local perspective or slice.

CO presses exactly here. If happening is real, then actualization is not free. It belongs to reality.

## Argument
1. The opening has already secured real processive occurring.
2. A fixed possibility-space by itself gives only what may be the case, not what is actually occurring now.
3. A completed timeless totality may preserve all differences, but it still risks demoting occurring to a represented or derivative feature of that whole.
4. So such views still need an account of how actualization, selection, or lived ongoingness is to be understood.
5. If actualization is unreal or merely perspectival, then happening is demoted.
6. If actualization is real, then the mechanism and outputs of actualization belong to reality.
7. Therefore a merely fixed possibility-space or timeless totality is not ontologically sufficient if happening is real.

## Why the usual escape fails
One might try to say:
- “actualization is real enough for us, but not ontologically deep.”

But this splits reality unnaturally. A real actualization-mechanism with ontologically irrelevant outputs is unstable. If it is real enough to occur, then its operation and products differentiate reality.

Likewise, if a law is said to govern change while remaining wholly separate from it, the burden has merely moved. The law must still constrain admissible continuation for particular local configurations rather than others.

## What this file establishes
- any ontology that keeps happening real must take actualization seriously,
- block or fixed-totality rivals pay a demotion cost if they preserve only structure while downgrading happening,
- and therefore the route may continue from change rather than from static possibility alone.

## What this file does not yet establish
- the full later theory of actualization,
- probability,
- or operator-level realization.
