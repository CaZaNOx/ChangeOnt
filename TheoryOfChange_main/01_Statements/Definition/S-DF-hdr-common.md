# Regime header family — live collapse/reopening estimate within an inherited embedding
## Claim (formal)
A common live regime-header state exports effective runtime tolerances and regime variables from prior settings plus unfolding evidence, tracking how much the current local field may be treated as collapsed versus reopened.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
The system carries a compact live estimate of how brittle, stable, or reopening the current regime is, and uses that estimate to shape later decisions.

## Philosophical Justification
[[S-DF-hdr-meta]] captures inherited stabilized embedding structure. [[S-DF-regime-shape-variation]] captures the deeper ontological issue that local unfolding can tighten, loosen, spread, or reopen in different ways. [[S-DF-regime-signature]] then sharpens this into an explicit local profile of operative invariants, burden accumulation, admissibility degradation, coherence, openness, and history dependence. [[S-DF-classical-collapse-law]] explains when that richer profile honestly collapses toward a thinner regime. Within that context, the common header is the current representational surface for carrying a live regime estimate. Ontologically the deeper reality is regime-signature and collapse/reopening dynamics; the header is the current interface-layer representation of that issue.

## Explanation (informal)
This is not variable birth itself, nor is it the same as metaheader. It summarizes current dynamicity, stability, burden/admissibility pressure, and reopening pressure so later modules know how strongly to trust existing closure, how much sensitivity is warranted, and when thinner collapse is actually earned.

## Derivation (Philosophical)
- A local solver inherits prior stabilizations.
- It still needs to track whether the current local region remains collapsed or is reopening.
- It also needs a compact surface for operative-invariant stability, burden/admissibility pressure, and collapse readiness.
- A compact live state for that estimate is therefore justified.

## Derivation (Formal/Logical/Mathematical)
```text
regime_header_t := g(metaheader, local_evidence_t, feedback_t)
```

## Clarifications / Further Context
- The term "header" is partly pedagogical and architectural.
- The deeper ontological claim is that change supports real mechanisms of collapse, brittle stabilization, reopening, and regime-sensitive thinning.
- Header should therefore be read as a downstream surface over regime signature rather than as the deepest home of regime ontology.
