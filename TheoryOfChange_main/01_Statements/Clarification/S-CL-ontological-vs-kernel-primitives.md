# Ontological primitive vs derived necessity vs kernel primitive

## Content
Only **change** is ontological primitive in the deepest sense. Nodes such as trace, recurrence, border, admissibility, or local comparability are best treated as first **derived ontological necessities**. Bend, gauge, and ReID are then later **kernel primitives**: compact reusable handles at kernel resolution for deformation burden, transport/alignment, and admissible continuity. They are not arbitrary design choices, but neither are they the earliest ontological distinctions after change.

## Implication
Whenever a file calls something a primitive, it should say whether it means:
- ontological primitive,
- first derived necessity,
- or kernel-resolution primitive handle.
Otherwise the chain silently compresses levels.
