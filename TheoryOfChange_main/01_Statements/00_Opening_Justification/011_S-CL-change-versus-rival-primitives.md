# Why change/happening is a stronger opening primitive than the main rival starts

## Claim
At the opening, change/happening is a stronger primitive than the main rival starts because it survives maximal doubt more faithfully, preserves the occurring character of inquiry better, and avoids the extra coordination or application burdens that many rival starts inherit.

## The test being applied
This file does not attempt a full history of metaphysics. It applies a startup test. A candidate primitive is stronger at the opening if it:
1. lies closer to what survives maximal doubt,
2. preserves the occurring character of inquiry,
3. does not require a second primitive or coordinator to explain happening,
4. and does not survive only by privileged exemptions or nullifying switches.

## Rival 1: blank existence / bare being
The strongest charitable version says: perhaps being is more basic than any particular happening, and change is merely one mode of being.

The problem at the opening is not that being is false. It is that blank being is too weak. The immediate datum is not just there-ness; it is enacted occurring. So being may be a later abstraction over what there is, but it is not the strongest first articulation.

## Rival 2: substance-first starts
The strongest charitable version says: stable entities or fields may be explanatorily prior, while change is what happens to them.

The problem is that this start introduces a second burden immediately. If substance is primary, then happening still has to be added as a further equally basic fact or interaction. That makes the start dualistic in pressure even if it calls itself monistic. Change-first has the opposite advantage: stability can be treated as a later articulation within change.

## Rival 3: mind-first or idealist closure
The strongest charitable version says: since inquiry is encountered from within experience, perhaps mind or appearance is fundamental.

The pressure point is that even an inwardly framed opening cannot get behind the occurring of experience itself. If experience occurs, then what is philosophically weighty at the opening is not merely inward content but the live happening that even mind-first views must still carry.

## Rival 4: multiple primitive starts
The strongest charitable version says: perhaps reality is fundamentally composed of several co-basic factors — for example being and becoming, or stability and change.

The pressure point is coordination. If the primitives are genuinely co-basic, then something still has to explain their relation or joint operation. If a further relation is added, the start multiplies again. Change/happening as monism avoids that burden if it can articulate stability and variation as aspects of one primitive.

## Rival 5: block or timeless-totality starts
The strongest charitable version says: perhaps the whole history exists tenselessly, and the sense of passage is simply one ordered feature of some local states inside that whole.

The pressure point is not mere inconsistency. It is explanatory demotion. A block can preserve structural difference while reclassifying happening as derivative, perspectival, or merely represented. Relative to an opening that takes occurring as minimal datum, that is a cost rather than a gain.

## Rival 6: law-over-state starts
The strongest charitable version says: perhaps primitive laws or fixed update rules explain the structure of change without requiring change itself as primitive.

The pressure point is application. A law does not escape the need for:
- a present configuration to which it is sensitive,
- a constrained space of admissible continuation,
- and a non-arbitrary way in which one articulation rather than another follows.

So law-talk often relocates the burden rather than removing it. If law and state-update are both primitive, the rival has not outperformed change-first parsimony.

## Rival 7: exempt absolutes and nullifier moves
The strongest charitable version says: perhaps some privileged source — God, absolute being, timeless totality — grounds what occurs without itself being subject to the same structure.

The pressure point is exemption. Such views often survive only by allowing what the opening refuses: a naked switch, privileged nullifier, or source that acts while exempt from the conditions it governs. Change/happening avoids that by beginning with live occurrence rather than trying to derive it from its own negation.

## What this establishes
- change/happening is not merely admissible at the opening,
- it is comparatively stronger than the main rival startup primitives under the same test.

## What this does not yet establish
- a full historical refutation of all rival traditions,
- or the entire later ontology.
