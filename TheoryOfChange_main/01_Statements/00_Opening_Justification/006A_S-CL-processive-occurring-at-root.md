# The occurring at the opening is already processive, not a bare event-label or static fact

## Claim
The occurring secured at the opening is not a thin “there is an event/fact” claim. It is already **processive occurring**: active taking-place, enacted now, and therefore already inside the broad family-sense of change.

## Why this file is needed
A rival can try to concede occurrence while thinning it into:
- bare eventhood,
- mere presentation,
- or a static fact inside a completed totality.

If that reinterpretation succeeded, the opening would have shown less than intended. So the route needs one explicit bridge: the occurring at issue is already processive.

## Argument
1. The immediate datum is not merely that some proposition is true. It is that inquiry is encountered as enacted taking-place.
2. A wholly static “occurrence” with no modulation, no onset, and no in-principle articulability would collapse into inert facticity or blank being.
3. But the opening datum is not inert facticity or blank being. It is lived/operative taking-place.
4. Therefore the occurrence at issue is already processive rather than merely event-labelled.
5. Processive occurring is already a lower-bound face of change, even before fuller state-transition structure is earned.

## Rival pressure and reply
### Rival move: “Occurrence need not be change; it may just be a fact/event in a block.”
That move succeeds only by changing the meaning of the datum. The datum is not “some item can be indexed.” It is “something is occurring now.” If the rival wants to reduce that to a static fact, it must already demote the active character the opening is preserving.

### Rival move: “The appearance of transition could be only content inside a static whole.”
Then the static-whole view has not preserved the datum as fundamental. It has reclassified happening as derivative appearance. The present route is entitled to resist that demotion at the opening.

## What this establishes
1. The opening datum is already processive occurring.
2. That processive occurring belongs within the broad change-family.
3. Rival views may still contest richer later senses of change, but they may not thin the opening datum back into bare eventhood without paying an explanatory cost.

## What this does not yet establish
It does **not** yet establish:
- full state-transition theory,
- metric continuity,
- or the complete later ontology of change.
