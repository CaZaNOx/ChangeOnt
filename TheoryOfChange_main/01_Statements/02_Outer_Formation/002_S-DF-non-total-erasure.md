# Non-total erasure — if some carried constraint holds, later unfolding cannot be a complete wipe of all prior shaping

## Claim
If a structured regime carries any influence or dependence forward at all, then later unfolding cannot be a complete wipe of all prior shaping. That negative result is non-total erasure.

## Why this follows
Carried constraint says that wholly independent reseeding is locally false in a structured regime. If that is so, then later articulation cannot be a total erasure of everything prior, because a total erasure would abolish exactly the dependence whose presence made the regime structured at all.

## Why this is still a weak claim
Non-total erasure does not yet say:
- how much is preserved,
- in what form,
- or whether the preserved aspect is content-like, structural, or merely shaping.

It says only that a complete wipe is incompatible with the structured-regime claim already established.

## What this establishes
1. Once carried constraint is granted, total erasure is ruled out in that regime.
2. Therefore later unfolding still bears some relation to earlier shaping.

## What this does not yet establish
It does **not** yet establish:
- positive trace,
- explicit recurrence,
- or recoverable content.
