# Experience — the lived occurring as given content

## Claim
Experience names the occurring insofar as it is lived as given content or modulation. It is neither the whole experiential structure nor a mere object. It is the given side of the same experiential whole already analytically split into subject, awareness, and experience.

## Why this distinction matters
The route needs to distinguish:
- the pole for whom/as which there is occurrence,
- the openness in which it is present,
- and the occurring as given.
Without this distinction, later doubts, memory, and semantic loading become harder to articulate.

## What this establishes
1. Experience is the given/content side of lived occurring.
2. The triad remains one structure under analytic decomposition, not three co-primitives.
3. The next file can now reunify the triad as one structure of experience.
