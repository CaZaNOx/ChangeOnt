# Regime signature — local profile of preservation, burden, admissibility, openness, and history

## Claim (formal)
A regime signature is the bounded local profile formed by operative invariants and their stability, burden accumulation, continuation-admissibility degradation, openness/closure structure, coherence, and history dependence. It names the current mode of supportable continuation, not merely a surface label.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
A regime is not just “stable,” “chaotic,” or “dynamic.” It is the current local pattern of what must be preserved, what strain is accumulating, how continuation is becoming easier or harder, how open the unfolding remains, and how much past shaping still matters.

## Philosophical Justification
[[S-DF-operative-invariant]] clarifies what preservation actually matters for continuation. [[S-DF-remaining-transformation-burden]] articulates unfinished transformation. [[S-DF-continuation-admissibility]] introduces supportability and its local degradation. [[S-DF-bounded-local-unfolding-operative-substrate]] localizes all of this to a bounded region of unfolding. Once these are in place, “regime” cannot remain a loose label. It must name the live local profile of preservation, burden, admissibility, openness, coherence, and history-sensitivity.

## Explanation (informal)
Two situations may both look stable at a glance but differ sharply. One may be lightly held, low-burden, and weakly history-sensitive. Another may be holding only under accumulated strain and strong inherited dependence. Regime signature names that deeper difference.

## Derivation (Philosophical)
- Operative invariants specify what must remain preserved.
- Burden specifies what strain remains or accumulates.
- Continuation-admissibility specifies how supportable continuation holds or degrades.
- Openness and closure vary with those same local pressures.
- History dependence specifies how much the line remains shaped by what has already occurred.
- Therefore the current regime is the joint local profile of these factors.

## Derivation (Formal/Logical/Mathematical)
```text
For bounded local unfolding x, a regime signature Sigma_R(x) minimally tracks:
(1) operative invariant set I*_R(x),
(2) invariant stability S_I(x),
(3) burden accumulation B_R(x),
(4) admissibility degradation D_A(x),
(5) openness / closure / coherence profile K_R(x),
(6) history dependence H_R(x).

R is identified by Sigma_R(x), not by a loose family label.
```

## Clarifications / Further Context
- Regime signature is ontological before it is representational.
- A later header or runtime summary may approximate this profile, but that is downstream inheritance, not the present basis of the claim.
- Regime signature should be read as a higher-order summary over operative differences and operative invariants, not as the prior source that makes them operative in the first place.
