# Stability and dynamism are two moments of one primitive rather than co-primitives

## Claim
If happening/change is primitive, then stability and dynamism should not be treated as separate ontological sources. They are two moments within one primitive:
- the ongoingness of happening,
- and the differentiated modulation of happening.

## Why this matters
A frequent criticism is that if change is primitive then reality must become pure chaos. Another rival move is to say that one therefore needs a second primitive—being, stability, structure, order—to hold reality together.

CO resists both moves.

## Argument
1. A primitive that only varies and never persists could not support any enduring route of formation.
2. A primitive that only persists and never varies could not support happening, articulation, or novelty.
3. Therefore an adequate primitive must support both continued happening and differentiated happening.
4. If these are split into separate primitives, a further explanation is required for why they meet and coordinate.
5. Change/happening has an advantage because the continued and differentiated moments can be treated as internal moments of one primitive rather than as externally coordinated co-principles.

## Why this supports monism
This is one of CO’s most important monistic claims.

CO is not monistic because it asserts a blank one behind appearances. It is monistic because the same primitive can support:
- persistence without requiring a second stabilizing substance,
- and variation without requiring an alien intruder called “change”.

That is what allows CO to avoid the “0=1 nullifier” problem. It does not need reality to jump from absolute absence of dynamism to dynamism by exception. Stability and dynamism are already moments within the same primitive.

## Objections and replies
### Objection 1: “Isn’t this circular—change contains stability because you say so?”
No. The argument is functional and comparative. If the primitive could not continue, it would fail to underwrite any later formation. If it could not differ, it would fail to underwrite happening. The dual moment is demanded by adequacy, not declared by fiat.

### Objection 2: “Why not just say being includes becoming?”
If being is really used that way, the disagreement narrows. But many existence/being-first starts still conceptually privilege static presence and treat happening as derivative. CO resists that ordering.

## What this file establishes
- stability and dynamism need not be separate primitives,
- change/happening can function monistically by containing both moments,
- rival multi-primitive starts face an extra coordination burden.

## What this file does not yet establish
- the detailed mechanisms of recurrence, invariance, or identity.
