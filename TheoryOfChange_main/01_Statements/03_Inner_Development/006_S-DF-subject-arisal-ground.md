# Subject arisal ground — supportable self-reentry of the recursive field
## Claim (formal)
A subject in the first richer inner sense arises when a recursive field does not merely recur, but can re-enter itself as *still-this-field* across successive breaths while remaining supportable under identity-admissible change. Subject arisal therefore requires supportable self-reentry, not a pre-given ego-substance.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
A subject truly begins when the inner field can hold together through change in such a way that later givenness can still belong to the same living center rather than to an unrelated flash. The point is not that a little object appears behind experience; it is that recursive lived holding becomes strong enough that a continuing “for-this-one” is supportable.

## Philosophical Justification
The recursive-field file already replaced the static ego picture with a bounded local field of re-entering lived structure. But a recursive field can still be too weak, too fleeting, or too fractured to count as an arising subject in a stronger sense. Subject arisal therefore requires more than recurrence alone:
- the field must remain supportable as the same field under change ([[01_Statements/02_Outer_Formation/016_S-DF-identity-through-change]]),
- and that supportability must stay within the admissible deformation range for that field ([[01_Statements/02_Outer_Formation/024_S-DF-identity-admissibility]]).

This yields a cleaner criterion than positing a ready-made self: subject arisal occurs where lived structure re-enters itself stably enough that later givenness can still belong to that same recursive locus.

## Explanation (informal)
A recursive field is the first richer picture of subjectivity. Subject arisal is the moment where that field becomes more than a sequence of similar pulses. It is when the field can survive enough change that there is a genuine continuing pole for later experience.

## Derivation (Philosophical)
- The inner triad has already been unified as one lived structure.
- The subject has already been reconceived as a recursive field rather than a static bearer.
- Identity-through-change and identity-admissibility state what it means for a field to remain supportably the same under deformation.
- Therefore subject arisal occurs when recursive lived structure achieves supportable self-reentry across breaths.

## Derivation (Formal/Logical/Mathematical)
```text
Let F be a recursive field over successive breaths b_i.
SubjectArisal(F) iff:
(1) F re-enters itself across breaths,
(2) later givenness can still be borne by F as still-this-field,
(3) the deformation from b_i to b_{i+1} remains identity-admissible for F.
```

## Proofs/Corollaries References
- corollary: stronger subject-formation can later support self-model evolution.
- corollary: once subject arisal is secured, minimal memory can be stated non-metaphorically.

## Clarifications / Further Context
- This is still not personhood, biography, or explicit self-concept.
- The file avoids making “subject” depend on heavy later machinery like language or deliberative agency.
- Threshold language can still be useful operationally, but the philosophical core is supportable self-reentry rather than arbitrary scalar cutoff.
