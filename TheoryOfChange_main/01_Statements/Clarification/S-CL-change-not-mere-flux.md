# Change is structured transformation, not mere flux
## Claim (formal)
Δ denotes structured transformation under continuity, reachability, and retained difference; it is not untyped mere flux or chaos.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
Change is not a meaningless blur. Even before full identity and invariants are articulated, unfolding already exhibits continuity, directional relation, and retained difference.

## Philosophical Justification
[[01_Statements/00_Opening_Justification/008_S-FT-continuity-noncessation]] rules out absolute stop-start fragmentation, and [[01_Statements/00_Opening_Justification/007_S-DR-change-cannot-begin]] blocks a first isolated burst of change. Together they already imply that change has connective structure rather than being sheer disconnected flux. This clarification therefore belongs before later identity-specific invariant machinery, not after it.

## Clarifications / Further Context
- When invoking Δ, specify the invariants (≈ thresholds, conserved relations) that make a transformation recognizable.
- “Flux” is permitted as variation, not as denial of structure.
- Ties into identity formation via [[01_Statements/02_Outer_Formation/027_S-DF-identity-invariants]].

## Explanation (informal)
To say change is structured is not yet to say that stable things already exist. It is only to say that unfolding is connected enough that later differences are not wholly unrelated to earlier ones. That is the minimum needed for reach, trace, and later identity to become meaningful.

## Derivation (Philosophical)
- Non-cessation means change is ongoing rather than atomized into unrelated flashes.
- If change cannot absolutely begin, then unfolding must be internally connected enough for earlier/later relation to matter.
- Therefore change is already structured transformation before later identity criteria are introduced.

## Derivation (Formal/Logical/Mathematical)
```text
If Δ is ongoing and non-originating in an absolute first event, then ∃ relational constraints R such that later unfolding is not independent of earlier unfolding.
Hence Δ ≠ mere untyped flux.
```
