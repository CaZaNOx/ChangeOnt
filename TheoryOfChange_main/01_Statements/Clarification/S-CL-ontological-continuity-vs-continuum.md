# Ontological continuity versus full continuum commitment
## Content
The active chain already earns a strong form of **ontological continuity**: change does not globally cease, so there is no absolute interval of stasis separating changing occurrences. This is continuity of occurrence or non-cessation.

That claim should be kept distinct from a stronger **continuum commitment**. A continuum commitment would say more than non-cessation: it would treat reality as fully continuum-like in a mathematically richer sense (for example with no atomistic gaps, no smallest parts, and a full continuous-space formalization already in place).

So the active chain should state the stronger point it has actually earned:
- change is gapless in the sense of non-cessation,
- point-language is idealized,
- local unfolding is indefinitely refinable or at least not atomistically justified,
- while stronger continuum formalisms remain downstream formal questions.

## Implication
Use continuity-noncessation as an ontological claim about unfolding, and treat stronger continuum mathematics as a later formalization question rather than something already silently proven.
