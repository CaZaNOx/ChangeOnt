# Stabilized co-treatment across variation — loaded distinctions begin to guide reusable treatment across varying occurrences

## Claim
A first-layer ceiling is reached when stabilized loaded distinctions begin to guide **reusable co-treatment across variation**. This is still weaker than concept-possession. It should be read as a stabilized guidance threshold inside subjectivity from which later concept-formation may become possible.

## Why this is stronger than stabilized association
Stabilized association can still amount to familiar recurrence. Stabilized co-treatment across variation begins only when the loaded distinction helps govern whether varying future occurrences are handled together or apart in a reusable way.

## Why this stops below concept-formation
Concepts are richer stabilizations inside subjects and belong further downstream. The present file therefore does **not** claim concept-like closure, concept-bearing structure, or language. It claims only that reusable treatment across variation has stabilized enough that later concept-formation becomes thinkable without having yet been earned.

## Why this does not depend on outer identity invariants
The threshold claimed here is earlier and weaker than the outer identity branch's recognition of preserved identity-content. What is needed is reusable loaded distinction and stabilized co-treatment, not already-recognized identity content.

## Argument
1. Stabilized loaded distinctions are now reusable across recurrence.
2. If they never guide treatment of varying future occurrences, they remain only familiar recurrence-lines.
3. Once they begin to guide reusable treatment across variation, they function as more than mere association.
4. The weakest honest name for that threshold is stabilized co-treatment across variation.

## Objection: "Is this already kinding or concept-like grouping?"
Not yet. "Co-treatment" is chosen precisely to avoid smuggling full type-possession, kinding, or concept-language. A later route may show how such stabilized guidance becomes concept-bearing under richer subject-internal stabilization, but that is not claimed here.

## What this establishes
1. Some stabilized loaded distinctions now guide reusable co-treatment across variation.
2. This is stronger than mere familiar recurrence or simple association.
3. It is a plausible first-layer ceiling for the inner route.

## What this does not yet establish
It does **not** yet establish:
- concepts,
- language,
- public symbols,
- or explicit reflective classification.

## Remaining issue
A later route should show how stabilized co-treatment across variation becomes richer subject-internal stabilization and eventually later concept-bearing structure. This file should therefore be read as a pre-conceptual ceiling, not as concept closure.
