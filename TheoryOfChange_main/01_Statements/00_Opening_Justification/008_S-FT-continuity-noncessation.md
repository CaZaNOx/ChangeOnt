# Continuity here means global non-origination and non-cessation of change, with only local regimes of rest or freeze

## Claim (formal)
From the impossibility of an absolute first change it follows that change cannot be treated as a merely local episode sourced by a more basic primordial stasis. A purported **first globally changing moment** does not remove the burden; unless it can explain how active transition is already live without brute stipulation, hidden static being, or closed repetition, it simply re-labels the problem. Therefore continuity here means **global non-origination and non-cessation of change**: there is no globally changeless base from which change begins or to which reality could totally drop, even though local regimes of rest, freeze, equilibrium, or repetition may occur within ongoing change.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
The previous file blocks not only the simple idea that change started out of absolute non-change. It also blocks the more evasive reply that reality might have had a first moment that was “already changing.” A first changing moment is not self-explanatory merely because nothing earlier exists. If genuine changing is present there, then active transition is already live there and still demands intelligibility. If no account is given, the proposal reduces to brute stipulation, hidden static being, or a self-enclosed loop merely labeled dynamic.

So continuity in this route is no longer only the thin claim that change is non-subordinate. It is the stronger claim that **global change is not originable and does not cease**, while local stillness-like regimes are interpreted only as local stabilizations within ongoing change.

## Philosophical Justification
If one allowed a total cessation of change followed by a restart, the restart would simply recreate the problem of absolute first change. Likewise, if one posits a first globally changing moment, one has not explained changing but merely installed it at the boundary as a primitive fact. That boundary move remains unstable for the same reason: a genuinely changing moment cannot be a static given with the word “dynamic” attached to it.

The opening may therefore no longer picture reality as:
1. pure global stasis at the bottom, and
2. change as an occasional derivative disturbance.

Instead, the burden shifts decisively. Global change must be treated as ontically live in the strong sense that a total drop to zero-change cannot serve as the real background from which change later reappears. What later looks like rest or stability must therefore be read as only local regime-structure within ongoing change.

## Objection: “Could there simply be a first moment already in motion?”
Only if that motion is genuinely intelligible as active transition. But if the proposal supplies no account of how that active transition is live there, it is merely brute stipulation. If it instead describes a perfectly closed repetitive loop, then the proposal has collapsed back toward static being under a cyclic label. And if novelty can emerge from that loop, then the loop was not self-contained after all. So the “first moment already changing” move does not remove the burden; it only relocates it.

## What this establishes
1. The route rules out absolute zero-change as explanatory source.
2. Change may not be treated as ontologically subordinate to primordial stillness.
3. A first globally changing moment does not provide a genuine escape from the no-first-change argument.
4. Global change is therefore treated here as non-originating and non-ceasing.
5. Rest, freeze, equilibrium, or recurrence may still occur, but only as local regimes within ongoing global change.

## What this does not yet establish
It does **not** yet establish:
- metric or calculus continuity,
- the detailed outer bridge,
- or a complete cosmology of every local regime.
