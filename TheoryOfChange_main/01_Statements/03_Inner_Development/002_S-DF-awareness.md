# Awareness — the openness or lighting in which occurrence is present

## Claim
Awareness names the openness or lighting in which lived occurring is present. It is not a second substance beside subject and experience, but another irreducible aspect of the same experiential whole.

## Why distinguish it?
If one speaks only of subject and content, one risks hiding the very presentness in which content is given at all. Awareness names that dimension of givenness.

## What this establishes
1. Lived occurring is not only “for a pole” but also present in an openness of givenness.
2. Awareness is analytically distinguishable from both subject-pole and given content.
3. The route can now reunify these aspects at the level of experience-structure.
