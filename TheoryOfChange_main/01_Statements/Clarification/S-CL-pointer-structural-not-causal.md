# Clarification — outer prior-relation is structural, not causal; subject-local pointer belongs to the inner line
## Content
At the outer stage, nothing literally points in the subjective sense. What is earned there is a **structural prior relation**: present articulation is not self-sealed and stands in an asymmetrical relation to not-wholly-exhausted prior conditioning. This relation is structural rather than causal and does not presuppose memory, clock time, or an already-formed subject.

Once a recursive subject-field exists, that earlier structural relation can become locally operative as **subject-local pointerhood**. The older symbols can therefore be read in two layers:
- `⇘` = abstract structural prior implication,
- `↶` = subject-local pointer/orientation.

## Implication
Supports using the outer bridge without implying that an as-yet-nonexistent subject is already pointing. Also preserves the stronger inner claim that pointerhood becomes licit once a local point of view exists.
