# DS and RDS are route-tools for a low-buy-in opening, not added primitives

## Claim
The Doubting Subject (DS) and Relaxed Doubting Subject (RDS) are methodological roles used to carry this opening route. They are not extra ontological primitives.

- **DS** names the thinnest enacted seat from which maximal doubt is carried.
- **RDS** names that same enacted seat once a small number of genuinely earned distinctions may be reused without pretending the opening has not advanced.

## Why introduce them at all
The opening is enacted. It is not a free-floating list of propositions. So the route needs some way of referring to the seat from which doubting, suspending, retaining, and later relaxing are carried out.

But it also does not want to import too much too early. If it simply says “person,” “self,” or “subject” in a rich ordinary sense, it has already granted:
- identity across time,
- memory,
- worldhood,
- conceptual articulation,
- and other thick commitments.

DS/RDS are introduced to avoid that inflation while still making the route enactable and intelligible.

## Why this is a route preference rather than a universal mandate
This project could have begun differently:
- by directly assuming that change is primary,
- by beginning from a richer phenomenology,
- by taking mathematics as the opening discipline,
- or later by appealing to empirical and computational success.

DS/RDS exist because this particular route wants very low buy-in from a critical reader. They are tools for that route, not universal furniture of all philosophy.

## Why DS is thin
DS should not be read as:
- a metaphysical ego,
- a completed self,
- or a full theory of consciousness.

It is only the minimal enacted seat required for the opening to be carried through under maximal doubt.

## Why RDS appears
If the route never relaxed, it would remain trapped in permanent suspension. But the chain needs a way to say:
- some distinctions have now been earned,
- and may be reused without pretending that the opening is still at zero.

RDS names that methodological shift. It is not a new entity. It is the same enacted seat under a changed epistemic allowance.

## What this establishes
1. DS and RDS are methodological devices, not ontological primitives.
2. DS names the opening under maximal doubt.
3. RDS names the same route once a few distinctions are genuinely earned and may be reused.

## What this does not yet establish
It does **not** yet establish:
- a full subject ontology,
- a rich self,
- or the later inner-route account of subjectivity.
