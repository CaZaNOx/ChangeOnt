# Point / Occurrence — idealized local happening, not atomic point
## Claim (formal)
Point(x) denotes an idealized local occurrence within unfolding change. It is a representational compression of a bounded happening-region, not an indivisible atom of reality.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
When we speak of a “point” in change-space, we are not naming a literal smallest dot of reality. We are naming a local happening under a chosen collapse or resolution.

## Philosophical Justification
- From [[S-FT-immediate-datum]] and [[S-DR-change-cannot-begin]], change is ongoing and not an isolated first pulse.
- From continuity and trace, happenings are encountered as local stretches or regions of unfolding rather than as frozen atoms.
- Therefore point-language must be understood as idealized shorthand for a bounded local occurrence, not as evidence that reality is made of indivisible points.

## Explanation (informal)
A point/occurrence is the neutral placeholder for “a happening here” once a local region has been collapsed enough for us to refer to it. It allows later structure (reach, eventlets, pointer, locality) to be discussed without implying that reality itself is built from atom-points.

## Derivation (Philosophical)
- Given change, there are happenings.
- Given bounded local access, we refer to happenings by compressing a local region into a handle.
- That handle is the point/occurrence concept.
- So point-language is derivative and idealizing: it names localized occurrence under a chosen resolution.

## Derivation (Formal/Logical/Mathematical)
```text
Let Ω be unfolding change-space and U a bounded local region under declared resolution r.
If H(U) marks a happening over U, then Point_r(U) is the idealized handle for that localized happening.
```

## Proofs/Corollaries References
- corollary: supports defining pointers and reach over localized occurrences.

## Clarifications / Further Context
- “Point” here is not an ontic atom; it is a local idealization.
- Different resolutions may compress the same ongoing happening differently.
- Subjective happenings are one special case of localized occurrence, not the only one.
