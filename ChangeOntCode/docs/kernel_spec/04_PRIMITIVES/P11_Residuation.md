## Role

P11 is not the deepest philosophical core. The deeper need is:
- remaining transformation / reachability / admissibility burden.

Residuation is one elegant candidate formalization of that need.

## Current status

- investigatory candidate
- not yet the unique or final default truth
- should be compared against alternatives such as reachability deficit, admissibility deficit, or deformation cost-to-go

## What should be implemented

If P11 remains active, the docs/code must make clear:
- what deeper burden/completion need it is approximating
- what alternative implementations are available
- whether P11 is default or comparison-only
