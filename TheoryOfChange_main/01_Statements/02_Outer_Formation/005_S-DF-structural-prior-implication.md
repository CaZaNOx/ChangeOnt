# Structural prior implication — present articulation is not self-sealed

## Claim
If happening is an articulated, overlapping unfolding rather than a sealed point, then each coarse-grained present articulation is not self-sealed. What is earned at the outer stage is a **structural prior implication**: the present articulation is not exhausted by its frozen face and therefore stands in an asymmetrical relation to not-wholly-exhausted prior conditioning. This is **not yet** subject-local pointerhood.


## Terminology discipline: “coarse-grained” here
The phrase **coarse-grained present articulation** is descriptive shorthand for a present articulation treated at the minimal resolution needed for this argument. It does not yet import the later coarseness-field doctrine or point-as-ball account. Those are derived downstream in [[01_Statements/02_Outer_Formation/022A_S-DR-shape-space-directed-unfolding-from-change.md]] after minimal adequate retention and thin collapse are available.

## What this means
Nothing in the outer route literally points in the subjective sense. No subject is yet present to orient, indicate, or attend. The earned result is weaker: the present articulation is unfinished relative to itself and therefore structurally implicates beyondness/prior-conditioning.

## Why this step is needed
Later files need an honest foothold for saying that current articulation is not self-exhaustive and that carried conditioning can still matter for what is present now. But the file should not pretend to have earned intentionality, explicit reference, or a subject-local pointer. It earns only structural prior implication.

## Argument
1. Contrast within now shows that coarse-grained present articulation is internally differentiated, not featureless.
2. Trace-retention shows that surviving differentiated shaping can remain active across overlapping unfolding.
3. If the present articulation is overlapping and boundary-like rather than punctual and sealed, then it cannot be wholly self-enclosed.
4. What is not wholly self-enclosed is unfinished relative to itself: it is not fully exhausted by its frozen face.
5. If carried shaping remains relevant to what is present now, then the present articulation stands in a structural asymmetry to not-wholly-exhausted prior conditioning.
6. The strongest earned outer result is therefore structural prior implication, not yet literal pointerhood.

## Relation to the older pointer language
Earlier FND files and recursive-chat work often used **pointer** for both:
- an abstract structural implication toward prior/beyondness, and
- a subject-local orientation once an inner field exists.

The active first-layer route now separates these.
- **Outer route:** structural prior implication / bounded bearing / local asymmetry.
- **Inner route:** subject-local pointer can later arise once a recursive subject-field exists.

## What this establishes
1. Present articulation is not self-sealed.
2. The route may speak of structural prior implication at the outer stage.
3. Later files are entitled to ask whether carried conditioning still bears on current articulation.
4. The outer route has a disciplined foothold without pretending to have earned intentionality or literal pointing.

## What this does not yet establish
It does **not** yet establish:
- subject-local pointerhood,
- intentionality,
- determinate predecessor ordering,
- a theory of reference,
- or a finished outer account of prior/reach.
