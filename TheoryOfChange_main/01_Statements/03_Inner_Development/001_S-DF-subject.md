# Subject — the analytic pole of one experiencing structure that carries the “for whom” of what is lived

## Claim
The subject is not introduced here as an extra primitive beside awareness and experience. It is one analytic pole of a single lived structure: the pole that answers to the “for whom” of what is lived.

## Why the split is analytic
The opening is dealing with one experiencing structure. But for the sake of leverage under doubt, it helps to distinguish within that one structure:
- what is lived,
- that it is lived,
- and for whom it is lived.

This is an analytic decomposition, not multiplication of primitives. The three poles are like distinguishable sides of one coin, not three separately introduced ontological starters.

## Why the subject-pole is needed
If one speaks only of content, the “for whom” disappears. If one speaks only of a bare self, the lived content disappears. The subject-pole keeps visible that lived occurrence is occurrence-for.

## What this establishes
1. The route may analytically distinguish a subject-pole within one lived structure.
2. This does not multiply primitives.
3. Later files must reunify the poles in the structure of experience.

## What this does not yet establish
It does **not** yet establish:
- a rich ego,
- a full self-model,
- or a mature subject ontology.
