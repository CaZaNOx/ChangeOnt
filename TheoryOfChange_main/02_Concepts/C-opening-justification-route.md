# C-opening-justification-route

This concept page gathers the **opening route of the first layer**.

Purpose:
- justify change as the strongest starting primitive;
- establish shared critical ground;
- attack rival starts fairly and explicitly;
- and mark the handoff into ontological development.

Closure condition:
A serious reader should be able to say:
- change cannot be responsibly discarded in critical reflection;
- rival primitives are weaker, more exception-laden, or secretly dependent on change;
- and it is therefore justified to continue by taking change as the privileged primitive.
