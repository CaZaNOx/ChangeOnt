# Change Clarification Route

This route begins once the opening has justified happening/change as the privileged primitive.

Its task is to slow the chain down enough that later derivation does not ride on semantic drift. The route therefore distinguishes:
- what is first secured as happening or occurring;
- why the word “change” is already a stabilized conceptual handle rather than the primitive in fully articulated form;
- what, at the root, is changing if pre-given substances are not yet licensed;
- why stability and dynamism need not be two co-primitives;
- how meta-change is still change acting on change;
- how CO differs from thicker uses of “process”;
- why block/fixed-totality rivals demote happening rather than preserving it;
- why no genuine eventlet can be a final opaque jump;
- and why the chain must use layered senses of change rather than one overloaded definition.

Reading rule:
At this stage, prefer the thinnest reading that still carries the argument. If a claim can still be expressed as a claim about happening/modulation, do not silently re-read it as already recurrence, process-structure, or operational transformation.
