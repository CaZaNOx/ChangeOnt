# Primitive — Arisal tension (precursor asymmetry / live strain)
## Claim (formal)
A local arisal-tension signal tracks unresolved directional strain before full order-asymmetry or variable birth is committed. It is a low-level precursor field: enough pressure to matter, not yet enough structure to declare a new stable category.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
Before a new distinction fully appears, change already feels a directional strain. Arisal tension is that pre-categorical pull.

## Philosophical Justification
- [[S-DF-delta-field-tension]] gives the basic notion of carried strain in a changing field.
- [[S-DF-subject-arisal-ground]] motivates why some asymmetries matter for later organized subjectivity or action.
- [[S-DF-change-fitness]] supplies the operational frame: tensions matter if they continue to bias viable unfolding.

## Explanation (informal)
This primitive is a live-strain proxy. It does not yet say what the system should do, only that some direction is pressing harder than the current closure comfortably explains.

## Derivation (Philosophical)
- Non-erased strain accumulates before it is cleanly named.
- When that strain is consistent enough, it can later support order-asymmetry, births, or switching.
- P0 therefore belongs before stronger ordering and growth primitives.

## Derivation (Formal/Logical/Mathematical)
```text
arisal_tension_t := f(local mismatch, carried strain, unresolved directional bias)
```
where f is implementation-specific but must remain a precursor rather than a full closure decision.

## Clarifications / Further Context
- This is intentionally weaker than [[S-DF-prm-order-arisal]].
- Treat it as a support primitive until its formal role is tightened further.
