# C-change-clarification-route

This concept page gathers the first clarification of what “change” means after the opening justification is complete.

Purpose:
- keep the root sense of change thin;
- distinguish that root sense from richer later descendants;
- clarify where CO aligns with nearby views and where it diverges;
- prevent semantic drift from carrying the argument farther than the derivation warrants.

Minimal root sense:
- At the opening, change means only: something is happening / occurring / not flat now.
- It is often safest here to hear “change” as **happening/modulation**.
- It does **not yet** mean: full chronology, memory, process-structure, recurrence, burden, or operational transformation.

Why this route matters:
- Without it, “change” risks stretching to whatever later files need.
- With it, richer later uses can be admitted without equivocation.

Critical note:
- The main conceptual pressure remains keeping the root sense thin while still showing why the later senses belong to one derivational family.
- The key anti-slippage test is simple: if a claim can be restated as a claim about **happening** without loss, it is probably still at the root stage and should not be treated as already structural or operational.
