# Subject as recursive field
## Claim (formal)
The subject is better understood, at the first richer stage of the inner route, as a recursive field rather than a static point-object: a bounded local region in which lived givenness, structurally prior-conditioned holding, and re-entry hold together strongly enough for a point of view to persist.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
Once the minimal triad is secured, the subject should not be pictured as a little substance sitting behind experience. It is more honestly described as a locally stabilized recursive field in which experience re-enters itself and holds together over cycles.

## Philosophical Justification
A change-first ontology cannot simply posit an ego as a static bearer of experience. The inner route therefore needs a way for subjectivity to persist without substance-first assumptions. The recursive-field formulation supplies that: a subject exists where lived givenness, bounded holding, structural prior-conditioning, and recursive re-entry stabilize together strongly enough that a perspective can continue through change. At this stage the older outer prior relation can also become locally operative as subject-orientation rather than merely structural asymmetry.

## Explanation (informal)
This file turns the minimal inner triad into a richer but still first-layer picture. A subject is now a field of recursive holding rather than a frozen point. Once such a field exists, the earlier outer prior-relation can become locally operative as **subject-local pointerhood** rather than remaining only structural implication.

## Derivation (Philosophical)
- The inner triad is already secured as one lived structure.
- The outer route has already earned bounded local hold and reach/local linkage.
- To persist, the inner triad must hold together across changing cycles rather than appearing as isolated flashes.
- Therefore the subject is better modeled as a recursive field: a bounded local region of re-entering lived structure.

## Derivation (Formal/Logical/Mathematical)
```text
SubjectField S exists when a bounded local domain U supports:
(1) recurring lived givenness,
(2) re-entry/reach across successive cycles on U,
(3) enough local holding for the same point of view to remain supportable across change.
```

## Clarifications / Further Context
- “Field” here is ontological and structural before it is mathematical.
- This file does not yet require a full self-model, personhood, or language-bearing identity.
- The use of bounded local hold prevents the subject from being treated as a global container.

## Next Steps in Chain
- derive the minimal conditions under which such a recursive field counts as an arising subject;
- derive the earliest memory witness and memory-trace integration within that field.
