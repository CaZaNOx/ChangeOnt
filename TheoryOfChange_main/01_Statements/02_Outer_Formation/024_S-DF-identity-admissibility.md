# Identity admissibility — identity-scoped tolerance before threshold
## Claim (formal)
Identity admissibility is the graded region within which a transformed bounded line remains eligible to count as one continuing identity-process before any sharpened threshold is imposed.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
After general continuation-admissibility is earned, the identity branch narrows the question: not merely whether continuation is supportable, but whether this continuation remains eligible for identity-claims. The first answer is still regional and graded, not yet a hard cutoff.

## Philosophical Justification
[[01_Statements/02_Outer_Formation/012_S-DF-continuation-admissibility]] secures the broader notion that some continuations remain supportable before identity-language is invoked. [[01_Statements/02_Outer_Formation/016A_S-DF-bounded-continuation-profile]] yields the bounded profile that later identity-claims must answer to, and [[01_Statements/02_Outer_Formation/016_S-DF-identity-through-change]] states the narrower question of continuity as same-line continuity. [[01_Statements/02_Outer_Formation/023_S-DF-similarity-operator]] then gives the graded retained-versus-altered comparison required inside that branch. The next lawful step is therefore not threshold, but an identity-scoped admissibility band.

## Explanation (informal)
Identity admissibility says: this transformed line is still within the field where same-line answerability remains a live possibility. It is weaker than recognition and earlier than threshold.

## Derivation (Philosophical)
- General continuation-admissibility is broader than identity.
- Identity-through-change narrows the field to continuity that may count as one line.
- Similarity provides the graded retained-versus-altered comparison needed inside that narrowed field.
- Therefore an identity-specific admissibility region is the next step before threshold.

## Derivation (Formal/Logical/Mathematical)
```text
A_id(P,K; C) ⊆ A_cont(P,K)
where membership in A_id depends on graded similarity conditions over the bounded line P under context C.
```

## Clarifications / Further Context
- Identity admissibility is a specialization of continuation-admissibility, not its source.
- It says continuity remains eligible for identity-claims; it does not yet impose a final same/not-same cut.
- Thresholds later sharpen the field when decision, reporting, or intervention require more collapse.

## Next Steps in Chain
- sharpen admissibility into thresholded identity-recognition where context demands it;
- then articulate the full recognition structure and the preserved content within it.
