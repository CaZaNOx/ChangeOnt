# Selective residue — if continuation occurs through local shape, some differentiations matter more than others

## Claim
If continuation in a structured regime occurs through local shape rather than beside it, then the surviving carry cannot be wholly anonymous. Some differentiations of the present local shape matter more than others for what continuation is admissible or supported. That unevenly surviving relevance is **selective residue**.


## Terminology discipline
This file inherits the thin sense of **local shape** from [[01_Statements/02_Outer_Formation/001_S-DF-carried-constraint.md]]: present local differentiation that can matter unevenly for continuation. It does not yet presuppose the later full definition of shape as relational-gauge/coarseness organization. The richer definition is consolidated only after reach, admissibility, comparability, burden, retention, and thin collapse in [[01_Statements/02_Outer_Formation/022A_S-DR-shape-space-directed-unfolding-from-change.md]].

## Why this is needed
Carried constraint alone says only that pure independent reseeding is false. Non-total-erasure says only that not everything is wiped clean at each refinement. But neither yet tells us whether the surviving carry is structureless. Selective residue is the next threshold: the carry is not just “something remains,” but “some local differentiations remain relevant while others fade, flatten, or matter less.”

## Argument
1. In a locally persistent structured regime, continuation occurs through the currently formed local shape.
2. If all surviving carry were wholly anonymous, then no particular local differentiation could count as making any difference to admissible continuation.
3. If no particular differentiation makes any difference, then one shaped present and another shaped present are equivalent with respect to continuation except by fresh imposition from elsewhere.
4. But a structured regime continues as **this** shaped line rather than another only if some local differentiations matter more than others.
5. Therefore some differentiations of local shape must survive as more relevant than others.
6. That uneven surviving relevance is **selective residue**.

## Clarification
Selective residue does **not** yet mean memory, a durable mark, or a fixed stored object. It only means that where local shape matters, it matters unevenly. Some formed aspects are effectively flat with respect to later change; others weight, constrain, favor, or inhibit further continuation more strongly.

## Objection: "Is this just current-state sensitivity rather than residue?"
If the current state's shape makes no differential contribution, then present shape is inert. If it does make differential contribution, then some differentiation of what is present is surviving as relevant to continuation. That is already enough for a first honest sense of residue, provided the term is kept thin and non-memory-like.

## What this establishes
1. The carry in a structured regime is not wholly anonymous.
2. Some local differentiations survive as more relevant than others.
3. This yields a first honest sense of **selective residue**.

## What this does not yet establish
It does **not** yet establish:
- that the same residue matters again later,
- that residue is stable over long spans,
- or that a trace in any thick sense already exists.

Those stronger claims belong only to the next threshold.
