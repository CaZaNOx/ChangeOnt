# Shape prior, formula, and evidence status

## Six-question prior status

The six-question shape prior is an active operational basis:

```text
hidden_decisiveness;
reshapeability;
local_cue_reliability;
revision_cost;
consequence_span;
topology_constraint.
```

Its correct status is:

```text
conceptually motivated;
currently active as a fixed operational regime descriptor;
not yet proven minimal, complete, or uniquely necessary.
```

The docs and code may use it as a frozen placement basis for architecture validation. They must not claim it is a final derived law unless later derivation/ablation proves minimality and sufficiency.

## Formula status

Scalars are allowed only as thin collapses of richer structure. Therefore every active scalar that affects readout must be classified:

```text
conceptually required structure;
implementation approximation;
empirical parameter;
provisional diagnostic coefficient;
inactive/inactive residue.
```

A scalar formula is paper-safe only when the docs state:

```text
what richer structure it compresses;
what inputs are allowed;
why signs/weights have their current direction;
whether coefficients were fixed before evaluation;
which diagnostics constrain it;
what claim boundary it supports.
```

## Evidence status

Architecture diagnostics can show that a mechanism is wired and behaves as specified. They do not prove performance superiority. Benchmark results become interpretable only after:

```text
boundary leakage audit passes;
relation/certificate traces are structurally coherent;
non-CO rescue selectors are absent;
formulas and shape controls are frozen or clearly swept;
fair baselines are specified;
logs show which mechanism caused behavior.
```

## Open watchpoints

Still open:

```text
six-axis minimality and independence;
exact scalar coefficients and thresholds;
shape-control ablations;
formula ledger completeness;
baseline literature comparison.
```

These are not reasons to stop architecture validation, but they must remain claim-limited.
