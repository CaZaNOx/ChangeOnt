# Bounded local hold — a locally stabilized coarse-grained unit of unfolding, not a globally privileged cut

## Claim
A bounded local hold is not an absolute metaphysical atom. It is a locally stabilized, coarse-grained unit within which unfolding is being tracked together strongly enough for recurrence, admissibility, and burden to become meaningful.

## Why this matters
The route needs local units of unfolding. But if it treated them as globally privileged cuts, it would reintroduce arbitrary chopping too early. The right claim is weaker: local holds are relative to tracked deformation, asymmetric local contribution, and to the stabilization actually being held together.

## Why there is no globally privileged cut here
If unfolding is refinable, then no single final segmentation is forced at this stage. Different coarse-grainings can be true of the same underlying unfolding. What matters is whether a given coarse-grained hold is stable enough for the next relations to be meaningful.

So the right question is not:
- “what is the one true ultimate cut?”

It is:
- “what portion of unfolding is being held together here strongly enough for recurrence, burden, and admissibility to be trackable together?”

## Why the previous step matters here
The route no longer needs a loaded local-prior claim to reach bounded hold. It needs only the weaker result that, inside a local bearing zone, contribution can be asymmetrical. That weaker asymmetry is enough for some local stretch of unfolding to hang together more directly than others.

## What this establishes
1. The route may speak of local holds without claiming globally unique segmentation.
2. A hold is a locally stabilized coarse-grained unit of unfolding.
3. This is enough for later recurrence, admissibility, and burden.

## What this does not yet establish
It does **not** yet establish:
- a final ontology of individuation,
- global metric partitioning,
- or a unique way of cutting reality into units.
