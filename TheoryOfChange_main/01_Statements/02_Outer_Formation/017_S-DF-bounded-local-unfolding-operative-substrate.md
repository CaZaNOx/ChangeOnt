# Bounded local unfolding as operative substrate
## Claim (formal)
The operative substrate at this stage is the bounded local unfolding currently held and updated by a local structure inside change. What exists here for comparison, admissibility, burden, and continuation is not a finished world-state but the local held patch of unfolding itself.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
The route has not earned a complete pre-given reality map. It has earned a bounded local patch of unfolding that is currently being held together well enough for preservation, comparison, and continuation to matter.

## Philosophical Justification
[[01_Statements/02_Outer_Formation/009_S-DF-bounded-local-hold]] shows that what is maintained is local and bounded rather than total. [[01_Statements/02_Outer_Formation/013_S-DF-local-comparability-field]] shows that ordered comparison already occurs within that bounded hold. Together they rule out reading the route as if it had already produced a complete world-model. What is actually available is the presently held local unfolding within which traces, differences, burdens, and continuations can be assessed.

## Explanation (informal)
This node says what the route is operating *within*. It is not underneath change like a hidden substance. It is the currently held local slice of unfolding itself.

## Derivation (Philosophical)
- The route has earned bounded local hold rather than total capture.
- The route has earned local comparability within that hold.
- Preservation, burden, and admissibility therefore apply inside a held local unfolding.
- Therefore the relevant substrate here is the bounded local unfolding presently maintained by a local structure.

## Derivation (Formal/Logical/Mathematical)
```text
Let U_loc be the currently held bounded local unfolding.

Then the operative substrate S at this stage is:
S = U_loc

where U_loc includes only what is presently retained strongly enough for
comparison, burden, admissibility, and continuation to be locally assessable.
```

## Clarifications / Further Context
- This is an ontological claim first; later kernel language should inherit it rather than define it.
- The substrate is not a substance beneath change.
- It is not an omniscient state-space imported from outside the ontology.
- “Local” is scale-relative: universe, organism, subject, or experiential field may each function as a local substrate depending on the layer.

## Active-chain status
**Status band:** strongly-derived  
**Reason:** bounded local hold plus local comparability already force a localized operative substrate; this file makes that implication explicit without importing thicker runtime doctrine.
