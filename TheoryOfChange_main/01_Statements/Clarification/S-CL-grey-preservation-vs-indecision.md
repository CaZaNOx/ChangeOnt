# Grey preservation versus indecision

## Claim
Grey preservation is justified when unresolved alternatives still differ in continuation-relevant burden, relation topology, quotient status, or collapse consequences. It is not ordinary hesitation under uncertainty.

## Not grey preservation
The following are not sufficient:

- uncertainty alone;
- lack of confidence alone;
- many action options alone;
- low score separation alone;
- generic refusal to decide.

These may trigger caution, but they do not by themselves justify preserving grey structure.

## Live-difference criterion
Grey structure remains live when premature collapse would erase an operative difference in at least one of:

- remaining transformation burden;
- public hiddenness relevant to continuation;
- branch relation topology;
- quotient/equivalence status;
- cancellation or relief status;
- consequence span of commitment;
- revision cost of wrong collapse;
- task-anchor relation.

## Collapse of grey
Grey may collapse when retaining further alternatives would no longer change the local continuation profile in a way relevant to admissibility, burden, relation, quotient, or commitment.

## Kernel consequence
A field implementation should not equate grey pressure with generic uncertainty. Grey pressure must be traceable to live non-equivalent continuation structure. If grey pressure is produced by scalar uncertainty alone, it must be marked as provisional.
