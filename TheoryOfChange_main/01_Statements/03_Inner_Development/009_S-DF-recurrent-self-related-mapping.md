# Recurrent self-related mapping — under memory-bearing recurrence, a weak self-mapping begins to emerge

## Claim
Under memory-bearing recurrence, a weak self-related mapping begins to emerge. The route does not yet need a rich or mature self-model. It needs only the first recurrent mapping by which the continuing subject-pattern differentiates its own continuity from merely passing content.

## Why the claim is weakened here
The stronger phrase “self-model” risks overstating what is earned this early. What is clearly needed is weaker: once recurrence and memory-bearing integration accumulate, the subject-pattern cannot remain wholly blind to its own recurrent variability. Some self-related mapping begins.

## Argument
1. Subjective unfolding now includes recurrent organization and trace-bearing memory.
2. Across multiple recurrences, variation is not encountered only as isolated content; it is encountered as variation of a continuing subject-pattern.
3. If no self-related mapping emerged at all, then the continuing pattern could not progressively orient itself across its own recurrent variability except as disconnected episodes.
4. Therefore some weak mapping of own continuity and variability begins to emerge.

## Objection: "Isn't this already too much?"
It would be too much if the file claimed a rich explicit self-model. It does not. It claims only the weakest recurrent self-related mapping that memory-bearing recurrence seems to require.

## What this establishes
1. A weak self-related mapping begins under memory-bearing recurrence.
2. This is stronger than mere recurring subjectivity, but weaker than a mature self-model.
3. Later inner files may build from this weaker result.

## What this does not yet establish
It does **not** yet establish:
- reflective self-concept,
- explicit autobiography,
- or a mature self-model in the strong sense.

## Remaining issue
The exact threshold between weak self-related mapping and a more proper self-model remains open. The current file therefore intentionally states the weaker claim.
