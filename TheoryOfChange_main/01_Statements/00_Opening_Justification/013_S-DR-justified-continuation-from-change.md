# Justified continuation from change

## Claim (formal)
The opening does not yet prove the entire ontology. It does, however, justify taking change as the strongest available working primitive for continuation, because:
1. processive happening survives the opening filter,
2. absolute first change is incoherent,
3. change is not ontologically subordinate to primordial stasis,
4. the main rival primitives are weaker starts,
5. and actualization cannot be treated as real yet ontologically cheap.

Therefore continuation from this point may proceed by explicitly asking what follows if change is primitive.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
The opening has now done enough work that the project may stop merely defending its starting point and begin using it. This does not mean every downstream claim is already proven. It means the inquiry is now entitled to proceed under the disciplined hypothesis that change is primitive, because no stronger available starting primitive has survived the opening pressure better.

## What this establishes
This file establishes the right to proceed from defense to derivation. The project may now investigate what follows if change is primitive.

## What this does not yet establish
It does **not** yet establish:
- the final sharpest meaning of change,
- the outer derivation in detail,
- the inner derivation in detail,
- or the later computational kernel.
