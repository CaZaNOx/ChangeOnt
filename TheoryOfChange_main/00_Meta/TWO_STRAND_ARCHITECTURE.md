# Two-Strand Architecture — shared opening, then inner and outer continuation

The first layer of `TheoryOfChange_main` is not a single flat derivation. It has:

1. a **shared opening justification route**,
2. an **outer ontological-development route**, and
3. an **inner epistemic-development route**.

These are not different realities. They are different derivational orders over one ontology of change.

## 1. Shared opening justification route

The opening route has one job: justify why change is the strongest available primitive.

It begins from what a serious reader must already grant under critical reflection:
- there is present occurring / appearing / doubting / experiencing,
- denial of change is performatively unstable,
- existence/substance-first alternatives are weaker because they can be posed statically and later smuggle happening back in.

The opening does **not** merely license the rest in a disposable way. It is the canonical place where the project shows why a change-first start is better than its rivals.

## 2. Outer ontological-development route

Once change is justified as primitive, one route asks:

**If change is ontologically primary, how do world-like structures form?**

This route derives and later clarifies:
- carried constraint and shape-sensitive continuation,
- selective residue, minimal trace, and recurrence,
- locality and bounded unfolding,
- admissibility, burden, invariance,
- bounded-continuation-profile,
- identity-through-change,
- regime and representation,
- eventual kernel-facing structure.

## 3. Inner epistemic-development route

The inner route must not be demoted to mere bootstrap.

After the opening it continues by asking:

**If change is primary, how do subjectivity, memory, loading, guidance, and later richer inner stabilization form from within?**

This route preserves the fact that consciousness cannot be exhausted by outside-only description. It begins from already-given experience rather than trying to derive subjectivity from a non-subjective outer line.

## Relation between the routes

- The opening route justifies the primitive.
- The outer route develops world-forming structure.
- The inner route develops the structure of already-given subjectivity.
- The routes carry different burdens, but once the inner route independently reaches prerequisites already shown sufficient in the outer route, it may explicitly reuse that outer result rather than rederive it from scratch.
- Later layers may introduce shared strands (logic, mathematics, relational operators) that increasingly rejoin them.

## Reading rule

When reading or writing a file, ask:
1. Is it part of the shared opening that justifies change as primitive?
2. Is it primarily part of the outer world-forming continuation?
3. Is it primarily part of the inner subject-forming continuation?
4. If it bridges routes, is the bridge explicit?
