# Δ(Now) implies structural beyondness; subject-local pointer is the inner expression
## Claim (formal)
Δ(Now) ⇒ structural beyondness/prior-implication. If a recursive local subject-field also exists, that same structural relation can be lived locally as pointer.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
If something is changing right now, the present is not self-contained — it implicates an elsewhere/beyondness relative to which it differs. That is the outer result. Only once a subject-field exists can this become literal subject-local pointing.

## Philosophical Justification
Change makes difference explicit. A difference without any beyondness collapses into nonsense; “different from what?” The present therefore implies structural beyondness. But literal pointing requires a local point of view, so the stronger pointer-reading belongs to the inner route.

## Explanation (informal)
Pointing in the full sense is not primitive at the outer stage. What is primitive there is that Δ(Now) cannot be fully self-enclosed. The subject later turns that structural asymmetry into local pointerhood.

## Clarifications / Further Context
- Elsewhere need not be an object; it is a position in the beyondness/reach structure.
- This does not add clock time; it only adds relational structure induced by Δ.
- The outer route earns structural prior relation; the inner route earns subject-local pointer.
