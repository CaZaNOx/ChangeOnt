# Opening Justification Route

This route is the shared opening of Layer 1. Its job is not only to say that change is somehow present, but to show why a disciplined inquiry is forced toward happening/change as the strongest start.

Canonical reading order:
1. root minimal stance
2. performative contradiction
3. maximal doubt bottoms out
4. minimal inference under maximal doubt
5. doubting-subject methodology (DS/RDS)
6. doubting-subject scope
7. immediate datum
8. processive occurring at root
9. change cannot begin
10. global non-origination / non-cessation of change, with only local regimes of rest or freeze
11. change versus rival primitives
12. actualization versus fixed possibility-space / timeless totality
13. explicit handoff to ontological continuation

Reading rule:
Do not treat the opening as already the whole ontology. Its job is to justify the start, constrain rival starts, and secure the thinnest live primitive from which ontological continuation can honestly proceed.
