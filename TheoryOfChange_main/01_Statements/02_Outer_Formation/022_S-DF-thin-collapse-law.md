# Thin collapse law — when richer change-structure may lawfully thin to a sharply ordered special case
## Claim (formal)
When the current regime signature shows sharply stable operative invariants, low burden accumulation, low admissibility decay, low history dependence, and little need for path-rich retention, the minimally adequate retained structure may lawfully thin from a richer change-sensitive form to a thinner sharply ordered special case.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
CO does not need to insist on its richest structure everywhere. When a local regime has genuinely become thin, stable, and sharply ordered, a thinner articulation may be the honest adequate form. In many downstream comparisons this will look classical, but that historical label is secondary to the thinning condition itself.

## Philosophical Justification
[[S-DF-minimal-adequate-retention]] already states that only the least structure sufficient for operative continuation should be retained. [[S-DF-regime-signature]] specifies when history, burden, openness, and invariant instability are or are not still operative. [[S-DF-operative-invariant]] specifies what must remain preserved. Therefore when the signature shows that richer retained structure no longer changes admissible continuation, that richer structure is no longer required. The adequate form may then thin to a sharply ordered special case.

## Explanation (informal)
This is not permission to sneak classicality in by default. It says the opposite: classical-looking structure must be earned by the regime itself. Flat, stationary, sharply ordered lines may justify thinning. Path-sensitive, burdened, or drifting lines may not.

## Derivation (Philosophical)
- Minimal adequate retention forbids keeping unnecessary richness.
- Regime signature says when the current regime is thin, stable, low-history, and no longer needs rich path-sensitive retention to preserve supportable continuation.
- If richer retained structure no longer changes supportable continuation, it is no longer operative.
- Therefore richer change-sensitive structure may thin to a sharply ordered special case under those conditions.

## Derivation (Formal/Logical/Mathematical)
```text
If Sigma_R(x) satisfies, within tolerance:
(1) high operative-invariant stability,
(2) low burden accumulation,
(3) low admissibility degradation,
(4) low history dependence,
(5) little need for path-rich retention beyond a thin ordered summary,
then there exists a thinner retained form Ret_thin(x)
such that Ret_thin preserves current admissible continuation as well as the richer Ret_rich(x).

In that case Ret_thin is the minimally adequate retained structure.
```

## Clarifications / Further Context
- This is an ontological thinning condition first; later algorithmic or representational collapses are downstream inheritances from it.
- It does not promote classical methods back to canonical ontology.
- It only says that a thinner sharply ordered form may be the right special case when richer operative structure has genuinely ceased to matter. In many downstream contexts that form will be recognized as classical-looking, but that is an interpretation layered on top of the thinning law rather than the primitive content of the law itself.

## Counterfactuals (refs)
- If no collapse law existed, richer CO structure would remain unjustifiably heavy even in thin regimes.
- If collapse were allowed without regime conditions, classical handling would become an unearned hidden default.
