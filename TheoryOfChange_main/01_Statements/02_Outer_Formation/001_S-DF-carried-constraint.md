# Carried constraint — in a locally persistent structured regime, continuation occurs through local shape rather than beside it

## Claim
The route does **not** claim that every conceivable region of change must display structure. It claims something narrower and enough: in any **locally persistent structured regime**, continuation is not fresh arbitrary reseeding. It occurs **through** the currently formed local shape rather than beside it. Therefore some carried influence or constraint must hold.


## Terminology discipline: “local shape” here
At this first step, **local shape** is used in a thin, provisional sense: the presently formed local differentiation by virtue of which one continuation is not equivalent to every other continuation. This file does **not** yet derive the full later concept of shape as a relational-gauge/coarseness field. That consolidation is supplied downstream by [[01_Statements/02_Outer_Formation/022A_S-DR-shape-space-directed-unfolding-from-change.md]].

So the argument here may rely only on the weaker claim that present formation is not inert with respect to continuation. It may not yet rely on CO-space, coarseness, dynamic shape update, or metric-like structure.

## Why this matters
A hostile alternative says:
- perhaps happenings simply occur one after another with no local carry at all,
- perhaps every apparent structure is just a fresh reseeding at each step,
- perhaps a global law explains order without any locally transmitted dependence.

The route does not need to rule out every imaginable unstructured region globally. It needs only this: **our structured regime cannot be of that wholly independent sort.**

## Argument
1. A locally persistent structured regime exhibits pattern, build-up, reinforcement, phase-out, or other differences between local formations that matter for how the regime continues.
2. If continuation did **not** occur through present local shape, then that shape would be inert with respect to admissible continuation.
3. If local shape were inert, then the difference between one present formation and another would do no work in selecting, weighting, prohibiting, or favoring what comes next.
4. But then structured continuation would be underdetermined relative to the present articulation and would have to be freshly reimposed from elsewhere.
5. Fresh reimposition is exactly what a locally persistent structured regime excludes.
6. Therefore, in such a regime, continuation must occur through present local shape rather than beside it.
7. The weakest honest name for that minimal surviving dependence is **carried constraint**.

## Why law-talk does not remove the burden
One may try to say that primitive law explains the order. But a law still needs a present configuration to which it is sensitive and a constrained space of admissible continuation. If the law is sensitive to local configuration, then it has conceded the core point: continuation is shape-sensitive. If it is not sensitive to local configuration, then it leaves local shape inert and cannot explain structured persistence. So law-talk does not remove the burden; it either redescribes carried constraint or fails to explain the regime.

## What this establishes
1. A locally persistent structured regime cannot be modeled as pure independent reseeding.
2. Continuation in such a regime occurs through local shape rather than beside it.
3. Therefore some carried influence or dependence must hold wherever such structure is present.
4. The route may call that minimal surviving dependence **carried constraint**.

## What this does not yet establish
It does **not** yet establish:
- which exact differentiations survive,
- how strong the survival must be,
- or that every local shape has equal relevance.

Those stronger distinctions are introduced only in the next files.
