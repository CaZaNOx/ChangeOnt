# Minimal subject-relative loading — bearer-relative forness, not mature semantics

## Claim
A difference is minimally subject-relative loaded when it matters not merely by prominence, intensity, or impact, but as bearing on the continuing subject-pattern itself.

## Why this is stronger than salience
Salience says: this stands out. Minimal subject-relative loading says: this stands out *for this continuing pattern* as bearing on its continuation, disruption, invitation, or resistance. The key separator is bearer-relative forness, not public meaning.

## Why the filename and title are cooled
The earlier route name foregrounded “semantic loading,” which sounded stronger than what is earned. The more honest burden here is minimal subject-relative loading: the first bearer-relative significance for the continuing subject-pattern.

## Argument
1. A weak self-related mapping now differentiates own continuity from merely passing content.
2. Once that distinction exists, some differences matter not only because they are intense, but because they bear on this continuing pattern.
3. That bearer-relative forness is stronger than generic salience.
4. The route may therefore speak of minimal subject-relative loading.

## Objection: "Isn't this still just strong salience?"
It may be redescribed that way from outside. But the claimed threshold is narrower: the difference matters as bearing on the continuing subject-pattern itself, not merely as bright or forceful.

## What this establishes
1. Some differences are now minimally significance-bearing for the subject-pattern itself.
2. This is stronger than bare salience, but weaker than mature or public meaning.
3. The chain may now ask whether such load can stabilize across recurrence.

## What this does not yet establish
It does **not** yet establish:
- language,
- public meaning,
- or mature semantics.

## Remaining issue
The main pressure point is still threshold strength: the route likely earns bearer-relative loading more securely than any hostile reader would call semantics. The file now names the thinner result directly.
