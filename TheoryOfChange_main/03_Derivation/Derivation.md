## 1. Opening justification
The opening route is not yet full world-derivation. Its task is to justify why change is the strongest starting primitive.

The opening secures:
- maximal doubt / critical bracketing,
- what remains under that pressure,
- immediate datum / present occurring,
- no first change,
- continuity in the weak sense of non-cessation,
- comparative pressure against rival starts (static being, substance, passive monism, over-thick process, fixed possibility-space, block pictures, exempt absolutes).

Closure condition:
- the reader should be justified in taking change as the privileged starting primitive and asking what follows if change is primary.

## 2. First clarification of change
The next route does not yet overload “change.” It distinguishes layers/faces of change rather than pretending that one overstuffed definition can do all the work at the root.

The clarification route now keeps apart:
- minimal happening / modulation / arisal,
- stronger non-beginning / non-static primacy,
- later structural and operative senses,
- meta-change,
- the stability/dynamicity dual reading,
- and comparative pressure against process if process means more than happening.

## 5. What is still open
The first layer is much clearer than before, but it is not philosophically closed everywhere.
The main remaining pressure points are now more localized:
- exact strength of some pointer/prior steps,
- later hardening of the inner route from early concept-bearing structure into richer communication and consciousness files,
- full graph/path alignment for all first-layer nodes,
- and later convergence work between inner and outer routes.

## Kernel bridge
The kernel map is justified only after the philosophical bridge is earned. Primitives and elements should operationalize already-earned distinctions rather than inventing them ad hoc.
