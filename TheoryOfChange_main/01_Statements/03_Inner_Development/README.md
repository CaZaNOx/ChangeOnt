## How to read this route
- The first three files are separated for clarity, but their ordering is **expository**. The first real inner result is their reunification in `004_S-DF-structure-of-experience.md`.
- `005` and `006` turn that minimal lived structure into a recursively supportable subject rather than a one-off descriptive triad.
- `007` and `008` earn memory in two steps: criterion first, integration second.
- `009` through `012` should be read weakly: they earn recurrent self-related mapping, minimal subject-relative loading, stabilized guiding load, and a stabilized co-treatment-across-variation threshold below concept-formation — not mature semantics, concepts, or language.

- Pointer language should be read carefully: the outer route earns only a structural prior relation, while genuinely subject-local pointerhood belongs on the inner line once a recursive field and local point of view exist.
