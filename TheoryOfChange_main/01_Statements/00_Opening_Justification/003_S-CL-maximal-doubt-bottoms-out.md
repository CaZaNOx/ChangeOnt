# Maximal doubt bottoms out at occurring experience rather than blank being

## Claim
Under maximal doubt, one may suspend rich claims about world, object, self, cause, law, theology, and even the authority of a full prior logic. One does **not** thereby suspend that doubting/appearing itself is occurring. The lower bound of the opening is therefore not a blank “there is” but a live “something is occurring”.

## What this claim means precisely
The present file is not yet saying that the full ontology of reality is experience. It says something narrower.

When one applies maximal doubt as a methodological filter, many **contents** may be bracketed. But the act of bracketing is not itself nothing. If doubting is occurring, then something is being undergone, lived, or enacted. That live occurrence is thinner than a world, thinner than a self in the rich social sense, and thinner than a worked-out metaphysics. But it is already thicker than pure blank being.

So the opening remainder is best described as:
- occurring,
- appearing,
- being undergone,
- happening-now in the thinnest admissible sense.

## Why this matters
A great deal depends on this distinction.

If maximal doubt bottoms out only at a static “something exists”, then the opening has already flattened the one thing it is trying not to flatten: the live character of the act itself. A purely static residue cannot explain why doubting is not merely posited but *happens*.

If, on the other hand, maximal doubt bottoms out at occurring experience, then the opening has already secured something closer to change/happening than to static being.

That does not yet prove that change is the final primitive. But it strongly biases the opening toward it.

## Why the stronger alternatives fail
### A. “Nothing remains”
If nothing remained, then there would be no doubting, no bracketing, and no inquiry. The act of maximal doubt would erase the very phenomenon by which it is being performed. So “nothing remains” is not an available outcome of actually carried-out doubt.

### B. “Only blank existence remains”
This is also insufficient. The act of doubting is not given merely as inert presence. It is given as occurring. A formula such as “something exists” may later be admissible, but at the opening it is less faithful than “something is occurring”.

### C. “A full subject remains”
This overstates what is licensed. One has not yet earned memory, personal identity, biography, world-knowledge, or rich agency. So a thick subject is too much. The remainder must be thinner.

## Argument
1. Maximal doubt suspends whatever can be coherently bracketed without collapsing inquiry.
2. In that exercise, one may bracket:
   - external world hypotheses,
   - object claims,
   - theology,
   - scientific background pictures,
   - rich self-descriptions,
   - and prior logical systems as authoritative wholes.
3. But the act of doubting/bracketing is itself occurring.
4. Denying the occurrence of that act while performing it is performatively unstable.
5. Therefore maximal doubt cannot bottom out at nothing.
6. And because what remains is lived as occurring rather than as inert blankness, the lower bound is better described as occurring experience than as mere static being.

## Formal sketch
```text
Let D = the live act of doubting.

Bracket(Content(D)) as far as possible.
Do not thereby erase Occurs(D), since erasing Occurs(D) while enacting D collapses D.

Therefore:
LowerBound(maximal doubt) ⊇ Occurs(D).
```

## Objections and replies
### Objection 1: “Why use the word ‘experience’? Isn’t that already too rich?”
It can be too rich if it means full phenomenological articulation, memory, subject-object structure, or concept-laden content. Here it means only what is lived or undergone in the occurring act. The file therefore treats “experience” as a thin term, not as a full theory-word.

### Objection 2: “Why not simply say ‘appearing’ rather than ‘experience’?”
One could. The important point is not the preferred label but the rejection of a purely static remainder. The file can tolerate several thin labels so long as they preserve occurringness rather than flatten it.

### Objection 3: “Does this already prove change?”
Not in the rich later sense. It secures a live lower bound—happening/occurring. The stronger articulation of change comes next.

## What this file establishes
- maximal doubt does not bottom out at nothing,
- it does not bottom out best at blank static being,
- it bottoms out at occurring experience/appearing in the thinnest admissible sense.

## What this file does not yet establish
- a rich subject,
- memory,
- a world,
- a process in the thick sense,
- or the full later ontology of change.
