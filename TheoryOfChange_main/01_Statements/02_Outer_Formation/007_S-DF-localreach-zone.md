# Local reach-zone — any such bearing must remain clustered and local, not globally indiscriminate

## Claim
If weak outer bearing-relation is admitted at all, it must remain clustered relative to a tracked unfolding rather than globally indiscriminate. The weakest name for that clustered bounded relation is a local reach-zone.

## Why this step is needed
Without locality, the prior cluster immediately overreaches. The route has not earned universal bearing, global topology, or an everywhere-to-everywhere relation. It needs only enough boundedness to keep later discussion local and non-mystical.

## Argument
1. Reach-relation says some not-fully-exhausted conditioning can still bear on current articulation.
2. If every possible beyondness bore equally on every articulation, then the relation would become globally indiscriminate.
3. A globally indiscriminate relation explains nothing about why this articulation unfolds with this structure rather than every structure at once.
4. Therefore any such bearing must already be clustered relative to what is actually being tracked together in the unfolding.
5. The weakest route-name for that clustered boundedness is a local reach-zone.

## Why this also addresses the law-application worry
A primitive law that applied everywhere with no locality-sensitive mediation would remain explanatorily thin. To explain why this local articulation continues this way rather than some other way, the update burden must be localizable somehow. This file keeps that burden clustered rather than magical.

## What this establishes
1. Any admitted bearing is bounded and local, not universal.
2. The route may discuss locality without pretending full topology or metric neighborhoods.
3. Later predecessor-sensitive language remains constrained to a local domain.

## What this does not yet establish
It does **not** yet establish:
- full topology,
- metric neighborhoods,
- or privileged absolute individuation.
