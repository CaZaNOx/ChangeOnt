# Boundary / public fact discipline

The translator boundary between a native problem family and the CO kernel. It may expose lawful public facts, constraints, action expressions, and public effects; it may not expose policy advice, hidden solver values, or optimality.

Canonical status: use this page as a concept index/orientation, not as a replacement for the numbered statement chain or kernel-spec contracts.
