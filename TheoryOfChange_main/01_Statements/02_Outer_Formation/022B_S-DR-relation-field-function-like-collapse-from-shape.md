# Relation-field and function-like collapse from shape

## Claim
A point, value, state, or function is not the primitive form of local unfolding. In CO, the more basic operational target is a **relation-field**: a developing, shape-conditioned pattern of reach, support, burden, ambiguity, and collapse-readiness between retained regions.

A classical function is a special case: it appears when a relation-field becomes concentrated enough under the current gauge that replacing it with a single-valued mapping preserves the continuation-relevant structure demanded by the local shape.

## Derivation from prior route
`022A` derives local shape, CO-space, coarseness, point-as-ball, and dynamic shape update. Once points are understood as thin collapses of coarse regions under a gauge, a mapping between points cannot be primitive either. What is prior is the unfolding relation between retained regions.

If a source region can unfold toward several target regions, the relation may be:

- open: several targets remain live;
- biased: one target has stronger support but alternatives remain relevant;
- function-like: one target is concentrated enough for collapse under the current gauge;
- strict-function-like: alternatives have no retained continuation-relevant difference in the current context.

Thus `f(x)=y` is not rejected. It is reinterpreted as a thin notation for a relation-field whose alternatives have become sufficiently weak, quotientable, or irrelevant under a shape.

## Probability and distribution
A probability distribution is not identical to shape. It is one gauge-specific expression of shape through a relation. Shape also includes coarseness, admissibility, hiddenness, projection horizon, burden persistence, relation density, collapse tolerance, and sequence sensitivity.

For example, an archer may still count as “hitting the target” while the distribution of hits drifts leftward. The coarse collapse remains valid, but the drift is shape evidence: repeated drift may eventually make misses more likely and force the collapse to reopen.

## Aggregate relation lawfulness
Some relations become function-like by pointwise concentration: one source usually reaches one target. Others become function-like by aggregate lawfulness: many individually noisy events produce a stable retained structure. A Monte Carlo circle estimate of π is not a pointwise exact mapping of each droplet; it is an aggregate relation whose inside/outside distribution stabilizes an estimate under a gauge.

## CO consequence
CO should preserve enough relation-field structure to decide whether collapse is earned:

- concentration: is one target dominant enough?
- ambiguity: are alternatives still live?
- boundary pressure: is the relation near a target/gauge boundary?
- outlier pressure: is a deviation noise, regime change, or collapse failure?
- aggregate stability: does noisy local behavior retain a stable global relation?

This is not a demand for a full probabilistic relation algebra everywhere. It is a collapse discipline: do not treat a relation as function-like unless its concentration or aggregate stability is adequate under shape.

## Boundary
This file does not claim that exact mathematical functions are invalid in their formal domain. It claims that, for CO, exact functions are idealized collapses of relation-fields. Formal equality and exact functions remain admissible when the relevant gauge demands and earns them.
