# Communication synchronization — outwardly carried distinctions achieve coordinated uptake across bounded local holds
## Claim (formal)
Communication synchronization arises when an outwardly carried distinction from one hold is taken up within another bounded local hold with enough preserved invariant significance that both sides can coordinate around the same-type distinction rather than merely undergo separate local perturbations.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
Communication is not just signal transfer. It is successful cross-hold uptake. One subject offers a carried distinction outwardly, another takes it up within its own local hold, and enough same-type significance survives that the two are coordinated around one distinction rather than merely triggered in parallel.

## Philosophical Justification
Externalizable distinction carriage has already secured that some stabilized same-type-guided distinctions can leave the purely inward loop and be offered into local reach. But communication requires uptake, not just broadcast. The receiving side must integrate the offering into its own carried trace and local organization such that the distinction becomes usable there as the same-type guide. Local reach provides the minimal condition that the offering can encounter another hold at all. Memory-trace integration provides the minimal condition that uptake can persist beyond an isolated instant. Communication synchronization therefore names the first inter-hold coordination of meaning-bearing distinction without presupposing concept-possession, mature language, public semantics, or full social institutions.

This matters because it closes another major gap in the inner route. The path should not leap from private stabilized same-type guidance straight to full language or concept-bearing stabilization. First comes coordinated uptake of externally carried distinctions. Then repeated communication can stabilize a weakly shared gauge. Only later can richer symbolic or explicitly linguistic structures be built on that basis.

## Explanation (informal)
A subject can now offer a distinction outwardly. Communication begins when another subject can genuinely take that distinction up and organize around the same thing, not just react independently.

## Derivation (Philosophical)
- Externalizable distinction carriage secures outward offering of stabilized same-type-guided distinctions.
- But communication needs a second hold that can receive and preserve enough of that offering.
- Local reach makes encounter possible; memory-trace integration makes uptake more than an instant flash.
- Therefore communication synchronization is earned when outwardly carried distinctions achieve coordinated same-type uptake across bounded local holds.

## Derivation (Formal/Logical/Mathematical)
```text
Let S1 and S2 be bounded local holds with memory-integrated trace structure.
CommSync(S1,S2,d) iff:
(1) S1 externally carries distinction d into a shared reachable zone,
(2) S2 takes up the offering within its own hold,
(3) enough invariant significance of d is preserved across the transfer,
(4) both S1 and S2 can now orient around d as the same-type distinction.
```

## Proofs/Corollaries References
- successor: shared gauge minimal and later richer intersubjective gauge should build on communication synchronization rather than bypass it.

## Clarifications / Further Context
- This is not yet full human language, proposition exchange, or explicit syntax.
- The point is the first coordinated uptake threshold, not a mature theory of discourse.
- Misfire, partial uptake, and distortion remain possible; synchronization here is minimal and graded, not perfect identity of content.

## Next Steps in Chain

- later ask how outer invariants and inner uptake jointly constrain intersubjective reality-formation.
