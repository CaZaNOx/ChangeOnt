# Self-propagating change and selective retention under admissible loss
## Claim (formal)
Change propagates by carrying and altering itself forward. Because propagation is bounded and local, what carries forward is selective rather than total: retained under admissible loss, not preserved as complete duplication.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
The next unfolding is not made from a second substance. It is change carrying some of itself onward while changing some of itself. A bounded local system therefore never inherits everything; it inherits what remains sufficiently preserved to matter.

## Philosophical Justification
[[01_Statements/02_Outer_Formation/003_S-DF-trace-retention]] already shows that prior change can still matter later. [[01_Statements/02_Outer_Formation/010_S-DF-selective-recurrence]] shows that not every aspect recurs equally. [[01_Statements/02_Outer_Formation/024_S-DF-identity-admissibility]] shows that persistence first appears as tolerated continuity rather than exact sameness. Together these imply that self-propagating change must retain selectively under admissible loss rather than copy total prior unfolding.

## Explanation (informal)
This node names the deeper ontological need behind later economy or compression formalisms. The point is not yet MDL. The point is that self-propagating change can only carry forward some aspects, under some tolerance, if local unfolding is to remain workable.

## Derivation (Philosophical)
- Change is the only primitive, so change can only propagate by carrying and altering itself.
- Trace-retention shows that prior change can matter later.
- Selective recurrence shows that some aspects recur and others do not.
- Admissibility shows that persistence is tolerated continuity, not total identity.
- Therefore self-propagation implies selective retention under admissible loss.

## Derivation (Formal/Logical/Mathematical)
```text
Given local unfolding U_t and successor U_(t+1),
U_(t+1) inherits only a selected, admissibly preserved subset/structure of U_t,
not the total prior unfolding in full explicit form.
```

## Clarifications / Further Context
- This is the deeper ontological issue behind later formal aids such as MDL, rate–distortion, predictive sufficiency, or quotient-stability.
- The present file does not privilege any one of those formal packages.
- It belongs to the canonical philosophical core more strongly than any specific compression formalism.

## Active-chain status

**Reason:** once change, trace, selective recurrence, and admissible continuity are in place, selective retention follows more deeply than any one later formal compression score.
