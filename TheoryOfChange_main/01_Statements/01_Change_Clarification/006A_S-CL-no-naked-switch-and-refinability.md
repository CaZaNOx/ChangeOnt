# If processive happening is real, no genuine eventlet is a final opaque jump; happening is in principle further refinable

## Claim (formal)
If processive happening is real, then the ontology may not treat transitions as naked switches whose onset is nowhere and in which truth simply flips without admissible witness. More strongly: no genuine **eventlet of happening** can be the final smallest happening while remaining wholly unarticulable as happening. Refinement does not remove happening; it reveals that the same happening is still taking place under finer articulation. Therefore real change is **in principle further refinable**: it cannot bottom out in a final opaque jump.

## Philosophical Translation (of formal claim)
A bare switch from A to B with no admissible onset is too thin to deserve the full weight of “happening”. It behaves more like a label change than an occurrence. But the point here goes further. If some local eventlet is genuinely happening, then whatever is happening there is not exhausted by a completed before/after description. The eventlet remains an unfolding, not a finished replacement. So when the eventlet is refined, the happening is not abolished; it is re-expressed at finer grain.

This means that a lawful discrete update does not by itself remove the burden. A law can specify allowed succession, but unless the transition is in principle articulable as transition, the law has only re-labeled an opaque jump.

## Core Argument
1. Real change is not mere difference between completed states, but ongoing happening.
2. A law relating completed states can specify allowed succession, but does not by itself constitute enacted transition.
3. If an eventlet is genuinely happening, then its happening is not exhausted by a coarse label such as “A became B”.
4. Refining that eventlet therefore does not erase the happening; it reveals smaller eventlets in which the same happening is still taking place.
5. A final smallest eventlet that was still genuinely happening but not further refinable would collapse happening into opaque replacement.
6. Therefore genuine change cannot terminate in a final opaque jump; it remains in principle further refinable as happening.

## Objection: “Could there still be a smallest lawful event?”
Only if the law made the transition intelligible as transition rather than merely stipulating that one complete state is replaced by another. But a lawful state-to-state rule still leaves the enacted happening unexplained unless the transition itself remains articulable as occurring. So the issue is not smallness as such; the issue is **ontologically opaque smallness**.

## What this file establishes
1. The chain may not rely on naked switches.
2. Real change cannot be reduced to before/after replacement with no transition-structure.
3. Any genuine eventlet remains in principle further refinable as happening.
4. Apparent discreteness is therefore a local cut, coarse grain, or regime-threshold, not a final ontology of happening.

## What this file does not yet establish
It does **not** yet establish:
- a full mathematical continuum,
- a metric or measure theory of refinement,
- or that every local regime lacks effective granularity.

It establishes only the stronger ontological point that no genuine happening bottoms out in a final unarticulable eventlet.
